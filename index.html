About The Book

Ben Davis was prepared for disaster. He just didn’t know it would come so soon.

He and his teenage son, Joel, are miles deep in the backcountry of the San Juan Mountains when high-altitude nuclear electromagnetic detonations light up the pre-dawn sky. In an instant, modern civilization stops. Ben, Joel, and their dog must make their way home—or to whatever’s left of it—on foot.

Without the ability to communicate with his family in Maryland, Ben has no idea if Joel's brother and sister are okay. Before their harrowing journey East even begins, they venture into town for supplies and discover one of Joel's classmates, alone and in desperate need of help.
